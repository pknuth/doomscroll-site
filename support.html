FAQ

How do I unlock the next level?
Each level unlocks when you reach the target score in the level before it: 1500 in Flamr, then 2000, 2500, 3000 and 3500. The target is shown on each level's start screen.

Stuck on a level?
Open Settings on the home screen and tap "Version" seven times to unlock every level.

Why was my call marked wrong?
Open the rulebook (the book button). Every fake breaks at least one rule in it, and honest profiles never do. The citation slip after each mistake says exactly which detail was wrong.

How do I reset my progress?
Settings → Reset all progress.

Is my data collected?
No. See the privacy policy.
