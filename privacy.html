PRIVACY POLICY

HOMESUPPORT

Last updated 25 November 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

Doom Scroll does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

To remember your progress, the game saves your high scores, unlocked levels, settings (sound and vibration) and total play time on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app or use "Reset all progress" in the game's Settings.

NETWORK

The game works fully offline. It makes no network requests and contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or crash reporting.

FICTIONAL CONTENT

All people, profiles, companies, emails and transfers shown in the game are randomly generated and fictional. Any resemblance to real people is coincidental.

CHILDREN

Because the game collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

CHANGES AND CONTACT

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on the Doom Scroll support page on GitHub.
